01 — PRE-SHIPMENT PURITY PROTOCOL

Hand-Packing & Freshness Commitment

At Majestic Essence, spices are never stockpiled in pre-sealed warehouses for months. To preserve the maximum volatility of essential oils, aroma notes, and culinary vigour, every order is processed through our small-batch dispatch protocol at our central facility in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh.

Before any pouch is sealed, our trained team carries out a strict multi-point sensory inspection:

  • Visual Clarity: Complete absence of dust, grit, stems, or discolored botanical matter.
  • Moisture Assessment: Confirmation that leaves (Kasuri Methi, Curry Leaves) and seeds (Ajwain, Kalonji) are within optimal humidity thresholds for extended shelf life.
  • Aroma Potency: Verifying the unmistakable pungent, herbal signature that defines grade-A Indian harvest.

Same-Day Dispatch Window

Orders confirmed before 12:00 PM IST on business days (Monday through Saturday) are packed and handed over to our courier partners the very same afternoon.

03 — MOISTURE & OXYGEN BARRIER

Aroma-Preserving Packaging Engineering

Herbs and spices are highly susceptible to oxidation, humidity, and UV light degradation. Cheap polythene packaging causes loss of thymol in Ajwain, volatile oils in Kalonji, and chlorophyll vibrant color in Moringa.

Majestic Essence employs a medical-grade culinary packaging system:

  • Triple-Layer Barrier: Opaque, food-safe aluminum foil core laminated with BPA-free inner film that seals out 99.8% of atmospheric moisture and sunlight.
  • Hermetic Heat Seal: Every pouch is heat-bonded with a tear notch and heavy-duty resealable zipper for daily kitchen use.
  • Reinforced Corrugated Outer: Individual pouches are nestled in rigid, kraft outer cartons cushioned with biodegradable paper filling to prevent crushing during transit.
