Tiny Seeds. Extraordinary Character.

The dramatic black jewel of Indian breads, pickling, and five-spice blends.

Majestic Essence Kalonji, also celebrated globally as Black Seed or Nigella, brings an elegant depth of flavour and a signature visual contrast to everyday cooking. Its dark, crystalline facets catch the eye, while its toasted, complex profile enchants the palate.

Offering hints of roasted onion, wild oregano, and black pepper, Kalonji is an indispensable element in traditional Bengal Panch Phoron, tandoori naan, artisanal sourdough breads, and heirloom mango achars. A gentle scatter over warm bread or roasted root vegetables provides an instant transformation.
