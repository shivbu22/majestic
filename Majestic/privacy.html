02

Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is strictly necessary to fulfill your culinary inquiries, provide authentic customer assistance, and deliver our products:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, delivery address, and company affiliation (if placing wholesale or sample kit requests).
  • Inquiry Records: Transcripts of voluntary inquiries, product preferences, and sample feedback submitted through our web form or direct messaging.
  • Technical Metadata: Minimal operational telemetry (IP address, browser type, anonymous referral source) strictly used to ensure website security, load balancing, and preventing malicious automated spam.
03

How We Use Your Data

Your information is treated with solemn respect. We process personal data solely for legitimate business and fulfillment purposes:

  • Order & Sample Fulfillment: Processing domestic spice consignments, preparing lab certificates of analysis, and generating shipping documentation.
  • Direct Client Communication: Responding directly to your WhatsApp inquiries, preparing bespoke bulk quotations, and answering culinary usage queries.
  • Product Integrity & Safety: Maintaining statutory batch-traceability records as mandated by Indian food safety standards (FSSAI).

We never sell, lease, monetize, or broker your personal data to any third-party marketing network. Period.

04

Third-Party Data Disclosure

We share personal data only with trusted infrastructure providers indispensable to completing your transaction:

  • Logistics Partners: Trusted domestic courier and freight partners (e.g., Blue Dart, India Post, DHL) solely for shipping label generation and parcel transit.
  • Hosting & Cloud Infrastructure: Secure cloud providers maintaining encrypted server architecture with strict access boundaries.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: If mandated by law enforcement, court subpoenas, or statutory authorities in accordance with Indian jurisdiction.
05

Cookies & Local Storage

Majestic Essence operates an intentionally clean, tracker-minimized digital experience. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels.

We may use temporary browser sessionStorage solely to remember your product interest (e.g., pre-populating an inquiry for Moringa or Bukanu from a dedicated showcase page) for your convenience during an active browsing session. This session data is automatically cleared when you close your browser tab.

06

Data Security & Retention

We enforce rigorous technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration:

  • All web communications are secured with industry-standard HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
  • Inquiry records are retained only for as long as necessary to complete commercial correspondence, settle transaction accounts, or satisfy statutory tax and commercial record retention mandates.
07

Your Data Rights

In accordance with applicable data protection laws, you retain the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation of what personal details we maintain regarding your inquiries.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact or delivery information.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of non-statutory communication logs once your transaction is completed.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at majesticessence30@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy Data Request”.
