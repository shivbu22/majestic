Sourcing & Terroir Origins

Authentic spices cannot be manufactured in a laboratory; they are shaped by soil chemistry, elevation, monsoonal rhythms, and generational agricultural wisdom.

Each of our five core botanicals is procured exclusively from its native geographic micro-climate across India:

  • 01 Moringa Leaf Powder: Organic smallholder farms in the Kaveri delta basin, Tamil Nadu.
  • 02 Ajwain (Carom Seeds): Sun-baked alluvial plains of Saurashtra, Gujarat, known for high-thymol seeds.
  • 03 Kalonji (Black Cumin): Rich black-cotton loam of the Malwa Plateau, Madhya Pradesh.
  • 04 Dried Curry Leaves: Rain-kissed slopes of the Western Ghats, hand-plucked at dawn.
  • 05 Kasuri Methi: Arid, mineral-dense desert soils of Nagaur, Rajasthan, famous globally for pungent fenugreek aromatics.

Purity, Shade-Drying & Safety

We view spice processing as preservation, not modification. Our goal is to leave the botanical in its purest living state.

Absolutely none. Our products contain 100% single-ingredient botanicals. We never use:

  • No synthetic dyes or artificial colorants (e.g. metanil yellow or Sudan dyes).
  • No anti-caking agents or flow conditioners like silicon dioxide or calcium silicate.
  • No chemical fumigants, sulfur dioxide, or gamma irradiation.
  • No added preservatives, sugar, sodium, or synthetic aroma oils.

Industrial dehydrators blow superheated air (65°C–80°C) over herbs to dry them in under two hours. This scorches heat-sensitive aromatic compounds, evaporates natural monoterpenes, and turns the vibrant green leaves brown and dull.

Majestic Essence herbs (Nagaur Kasuri Methi and Western Ghats Curry Leaves) undergo slow, natural shadow-drying in temperature-moderated pavilions with filtered circulating air. This locks in natural chlorophyll, retains structural cell integrity, and captures the unmistakable herbal punch that blossoms the second you crush the leaves between your palms.

Yes. All operations, hand-sorting, and packaging protocols strictly adhere to the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) regulatory framework, maintaining cleanroom hygienic conditions and food-grade packaging standards.

Kitchen Storage & Freshness Tips

How you care for your spices in your home kitchen determines how long their volatile oils remain vibrant.

Our pouches are manufactured with multi-layer aluminum foil barriers and heavy-duty resealable zipper locks. To maintain maximum potency:

  1. Squeeze excess air out of the pouch before pressing the zip seal tight.
  2. Store in a cool, dark cabinet or drawer away from stovetop steam, ovens, and direct sunlight.
  3. Never hold an open pouch directly over a steaming pot of curry, as vapor entering the bag induces mold and accelerates oil breakdown.
  4. Always use a clean, bone-dry wooden or stainless steel spoon.

Yes! We recommend amber glass, ceramic, or stainless steel airtight jars. Avoid clear glass containers kept on open shelves near windows, as UV rays rapidly degrade herbal nutrients and fade natural colors.

Sealed, our triple-layer barrier pouches retain maximum therapeutic and aromatic potency for 12 months from the packing date. Once opened, we advise enjoying the spices within 3 to 6 months to experience their highest aromatic peak.
