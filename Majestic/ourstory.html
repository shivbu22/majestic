Foundational Conviction

Quality Should Never Be Complicated.

It shouldn't require deciphering cryptic industry grades or second-guessing authenticity. Quality should be something you can see with your eyes, smell the instant you break the seal, taste in the first spoonful of your dal, and trust every single day.

In Indian culinary culture, spices have never been mere commodities. They are the essence of the home—the aroma that awakens family members in the morning, the healing warmth brewed into winter decoctions, and the golden memory carried from grandmothers to future generations.

Botanical Purity Standard • Executive Summary

What Defines Majestic Essence Spices?

Majestic Essence is an artisanal botanical house headquartered in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, founded by Pranav Pratap Singh. The house specializes in single-origin, small-batch whole herbs and heritage culinary formulations—including its flagship Bukanu digestive masala, Nagaur Kasuri Methi, Saurashtra Ajwain, Malwa Kalonji, Western Ghats Curry Leaves, and Kaveri Basin Moringa. Every offering is verified under FSSAI standards, free from synthetic colorants, starch fillers, and artificial aromas.

I

Direct Origin Selection

We work with growers in regions naturally celebrated for specific herbs. From Nagaur's arid fenugreek fields to Saurashtra's coastal ajwain belts.

II

Traditional Gentle Drying

No high-heat artificial kilns that bake out volatile oils. Ingredients are gently dried to preserve vibrant chlorophyll and essential aromatics.

III

Rigorous De-Stonning & Grading

Multi-tier aspiration and visual sorting ensure every pack is free from dust, foreign stems, stones, or substandard broken seeds.

IV

Sealed for Freshness

Sealed in multi-layer food-grade barrier pouches that block ultraviolet light, atmospheric humidity, and oxygen degradation.
